Why are payroll costs in the London office 12% over budget this quarter?
Reasoning across 4 modules…

The variance has three contributing factors, ranked by impact:

  • Unbudgeted hires (HiTalent) — 4 senior engineers were onboarded in March without updated budget allocations, adding £48,000/mo
  • Contractor overtime (HiOps) — 3 contractors exceeded their 40-hr caps by an average of 18 hrs/wk, adding £12,400/mo
  • FX fluctuation (HiPay) — GBP→USD rate shifted 3.2%, impacting USD-denominated vendor payments by £8,200/mo

Recommended action: Cap contractor overtime immediately (saves £12.4K/mo). Retroactively adjust Q2 budget in HiPay to account for the 4 hires. Hedge FX exposure for Q3.
